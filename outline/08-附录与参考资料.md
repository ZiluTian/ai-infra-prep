## 附录 A:公式速查(目标 1 页)
- KV Cache:`KV/token = 2 × L × h_kv × d_head × bytes`
- 权重体积:`params × bytes`
- TP 通信量:`2 × bs × seq × hidden × bytes`(每层 ×2 次 all-reduce)
- EP all-to-all:`bs × seq × topk × hidden × bytes / N`
- PP 点对点:`micro_bs × hidden × bytes × stages`
- Roofline:`time = max(FLOPs / peak_FLOPS, bytes / peak_BW)`
- 投机解码净加速(含验证开销的期望式)
- 容量:`卡数 = (λ × L_out) / 单卡吞吐 × 余量`
